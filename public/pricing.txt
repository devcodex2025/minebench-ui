# MineBench pricing

## Free client and public results
Download the client and browse public benchmark results without buying Premium.
Download: https://minebench.cloud/downloads

## Premium
Price: 29 USDC once, paid on Solana. The checkout describes a one-time payment.
Additional costs: SOL network fees. The current checkout requires at least 0.005 SOL available; this is not a fixed fee.
Requires: a Solana wallet with USDC and SOL. Review the transaction in your wallet before approving payment.
Current features and checkout: https://minebench.cloud/premium
Payout conditions depend on the pool. No hashrate or earnings guarantee.
Support: https://discord.gg/vsDyYh4rma
